Welcome to Iftekhar Lab

Research in food engineering, tea science, food safety, and intelligent processing.

Our research interests center on understanding and improving food quality and safety through experimental food science, process engineering, and data-informed methods. We are interested in practical approaches that connect laboratory measurements with food processing challenges.

Based in the Department of Food Engineering and Tea Technology at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), Iftekhar Lab aims to contribute to research and innovation relevant to Bangladesh's food and tea sectors.

Research Focus

  1. Food Processing, Quality and Shelf Life

    Studying processing conditions and their relationships with food quality, stability, and product characteristics.

  2. Tea Science and Quality Improvement

    Exploring tea blending, analytical and sensory evaluation, quality characterization, and potential value recovery from off-grade tea materials.

  3. AI-Enabled Sensing and Process Optimization

    Investigating how machine vision, sensor measurements, and machine-learning methods can support material classification, quality prediction, and process optimization.

  4. Food Safety and Sustainable Processing

    Connecting food safety assessment and process validation with resource efficiency, by-product valorization, and more sustainable processing strategies.

Research Directions

Smart reconditioning of off-grade tea

One research direction of interest is the use of sensor-based characterization and controlled processing to study whether selected off-grade teas can be improved in quality and market value. A potential workflow combines raw-material characterization, moisture conditioning, process optimization, and analytical and sensory validation.
